Auto-Renewal Service Rules

These rules shall be regarded as a supplementary agreement to the Membership Service Agreement (if the link address is changed, the content corresponding to the updated link shall prevail; for any other changes of link addresses, the foregoing provisions shall apply), and form an inseparable part thereof, constituting a unified whole together with it.
In the event of any conflict between these rules and the Membership Service Agreement, these rules shall prevail.
By clicking Subscribe, activating, and using the membership auto-renewal service, you are deemed to have agreed to these rules.

You understand and agree that:
1. This service is an auto-renewal service provided on the premise that you have already activated the membership service. If you activate this service, when your membership service is about to expire, the service fee for the next billing cycle will be automatically deducted from the balance of your own recharge account, linked third-party payment account, bank card, telecom account, iTunes account, and other accounts (collectively referred to as the “Account”). Billing cycles include but are not limited to monthly, quarterly, and annual cycles. The implementation of this service is subject to your activation and linkage of the Account and successful deduction from the above Account(s). Any risks and/or losses such as renewal failure due to insufficient deductible balance in the above Account(s) shall be borne by you.

2. Subscription periods include but are not limited to one month, three months, six months, and one year.

3. After purchasing the auto-renewal service, the fee will be automatically charged from your iTunes account 24 hours before the end of each billing cycle, and the membership validity for the corresponding billing cycle will be extended accordingly.

4. For auto-renewal users who pay via Apple In-App Purchase (IAP, in-app purchase), the unsubscribe method is as follows: open the "Settings" menu on your iPhone, enter "iTunes Store & App Store", tap "Apple ID", choose "View Apple ID", enter the "Account Settings" page, tap "Subscriptions", and select the app membership to cancel the subscription. If the auto-renewal function is not canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the billing cycle, it will automatically renew and charge you.

5. About the "Restore Purchases (RESTORE PURCHASES)" button: it means that after you reinstall the app or change devices, you can restore the in-app products and services you have purchased. This process usually involves syncing your purchase records to ensure that you can continue using purchased products and services on a new device without repurchasing. If you still have an active subscription to this app, the app will be restored to an available state.
